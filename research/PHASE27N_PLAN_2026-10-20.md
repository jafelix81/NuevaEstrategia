# Phase 27N — SEC N-PORT PIT institutional-flow pilot

## Gate from 27M
27M completed successfully and showed that the N-PORT identifier problem is technically resolvable enough for a controlled pilot: exact frozen-universe ticker coverage was 267–324 names by snapshot, while same-snapshot unique CUSIP candidate links covered 329–365 securities and had zero CUSIP conflicts in all eight snapshots; ISIN conflicts were zero except one 2022Q4 case. These are PIT candidate links only, not permanent identity.

## Pre-registered pilot
- Frozen universe: exactly 335 names from Phase 0; no expansion.
- Snapshot rule: latest filing per SERIES_ID for the quarter-end report date, limited to filings no later than report date + 75 calendar days.
- Security resolution, in order: exact frozen-universe ticker; otherwise unique CUSIP observed with an exact frozen-universe ticker in the same snapshot; otherwise unique ISIN observed with an exact frozen-universe ticker in the same snapshot. Ambiguities are excluded.
- Signal: aggregate reported shares by frozen ticker across selected funds. Compare to the immediately prior snapshot. Require positive shares in both snapshots and prior aggregate reported market value >= $50m. Rank the cross-section by share-growth rate; long top decile and short bottom decile, equal weight.
- OOS entry: first available adjusted close strictly after the snapshot knowledge cutoff. Holding horizon: 63 trading days. No same-day return and no use of future mappings or future prices in formation.
- 2026Q3 is signal-only because a complete 63-trading-day OOS horizon is not yet available at the run date.

## Interpretation gate
This is a pilot, not a production candidate. A positive aggregate result is not sufficient by itself. A subsequent walk-forward/stability test is required before any baseline change. If the flow signal is weak, unstable, or materially dependent on a small number of periods/names, the hypothesis should be killed rather than optimized.
