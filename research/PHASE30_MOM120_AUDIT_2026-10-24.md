## Decision

**INVALID — do not use the current Phase 30 portfolio results as evidence for or against MOM120 alpha.**

## Why

The artifact declares a Top-20 / PARTIAL50 protocol, but the resulting ledger has approximately **193–198 average positions** and the final day has **201 positions**. This is incompatible with a Top-20 portfolio unless the implementation is intentionally carrying a large residual book; no such exposure rule is documented in the protocol.

The artifact also reports an internally inconsistent economic profile: final equity multiples around 3.4–3.5x over 2024-01-02 through 2026-10-23, while mean daily returns are slightly negative and annualized volatility is implausibly tiny. The daily ledger confirms equity moved from 1,000 to roughly 38,256 in the MOM120 C20 run, so the reported performance statistics cannot be trusted until the return-accounting/valuation convention is independently reconciled.

## Evidence

- `phase30_report.json`: `top_n=20`, `partial=0.5`, `max_position=0.2`, `gross_cap=1.0`, signal convention `close t -> open t+1`.
- Same report: MOM120 C20 `avg_positions=193.2819`, `final_multiple=3.4607`, `CAGR=55.76%`, `Sharpe=-1.684`.
- `MOM120_ledger_C20.csv`: 2,718 rows from 2016-01-04 to 2026-10-23; final equity `38,255.56`; final `Positions=201`.
- `MOM120_trades_C20.csv`: 4,239 trade rows, showing the implementation is carrying a much larger book than the stated Top-20 protocol.

## Required correction before any promotion

Rebuild the economic validation from scratch with an explicit state machine:

1. Signal is formed at **Close(t)** only.
2. Entry/rebalance executes at **Open(t+1)**.
3. Portfolio valuation uses **Close(t)** after applying that day's price movement.
4. At every rebalance, holdings must be reconciled explicitly to the target Top-20/PARTIAL50 state; stale holdings cannot persist outside the documented rule.
5. Gross exposure, position count, cash, transaction costs and equity must satisfy accounting identities every day.
6. Return statistics must be computed directly from the audited equity series, not from a separately transformed return series.
7. Re-run the same OOS dates, same universe, same costs and same F80/LTM control before interpreting MOM120.
8. Report bootstrap and LOO on the **daily excess return series** only after the accounting audit passes.

Until these checks pass, **MOM120 remains an independent cross-sectional hypothesis, not an investable strategy or a proven incremental factor**.
