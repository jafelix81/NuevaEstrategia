# Phase 435 — Compass Item 408 plan-cap / Form 4 / Form 144 execution reconciliation

**Date:** 2026-11-16  
**Status:** one-issuer source-semantic case study completed; no alpha admission  
**Scope:** Compass, Inc. (COMP), one 2024 Rule 10b5-1 plan disclosed in a Form 10-Q and reconciled to selected Form 4 records and a later Form 144.

## Executive result

A second issuer case confirms that Item 408 can disclose a material potential-supply ceiling before subsequent reported sales, but the public disclosure may arrive long after plan adoption and only shortly before execution begins.

Compass's 2024-06-30 Form 10-Q, filed **2024-08-02**, disclosed a plan adopted **2024-05-10** by CEO Robert Reffkin and the 2021 Reffkin Remainder Interest Trust for sales of **up to 10,500,000 shares**, expiring 2025-12-31. The issuer reported 505,199,755 shares outstanding as of 2024-07-29. The plan ceiling therefore represented approximately **2.0784%** of shares outstanding.

The later Form 144, filed 2024-11-04, lists nine sales from 2024-08-12 through 2024-09-10: **3,000,000 direct Reffkin shares plus 500,000 trust shares, 3,500,000 total**, with gross proceeds of $17,131,613. That is **33.3333%** of the disclosed plan ceiling and approximately **0.6928%** of the July 29 shares-outstanding figure.

The first listed sale occurred **10 calendar days** after the 10-Q filing. There were five full intervening trading sessions (Aug. 5–9); the first sale occurred on the sixth post-filing session (Aug. 12). The plan had been adopted 84 calendar days before the public 10-Q filing. Thus the adoption date materially predates public availability, while the usable public lead in this case was short.

This is useful source-feasibility evidence, not proof of price impact or predictive alpha.

## Point-in-time implications

1. The plan adoption date (May 10) is not the signal date. The 10-Q filing date (Aug. 2) is the conservative public-information date.
2. For a close-to-close/daily strategy, this report uses the next regular session, Aug. 5, as the first eligible session. The directory listing's 16:26:06 ET modified-time proxy is not treated as certified first-public receipt.
3. The first sale on Aug. 12 followed five complete intervening sessions. A plan may therefore have limited public lead even when the plan itself was adopted months earlier.
4. The disclosed 10.5m is a maximum. The 3.5m later reported sales do not establish that the remaining 7.0m was still active, executable, or intended to be sold.
5. The later Form 144 is a retrospective reconciliation source, not a leading signal.
6. The plan includes a trust. Direct and indirect ownership must remain separate until identity and beneficial-ownership semantics are resolved.

**Decision:** retain Item 408 as a conditional potential-supply / overhang source. The case shows a large disclosed ceiling followed by reported sales, with only a five-session public lead before the first sale. It does not establish that the disclosure predicts returns, that all plan capacity is executed, or that plan sales cause price pressure.
