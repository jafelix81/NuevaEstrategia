- **Root cause:** FINRA's official User Guide shows multiple rows for the same security across Trade Facilities; Market is a one-letter facility code. The parser incorrectly enforced symbol-only uniqueness and treated Market as optional. Official sources: [FINRA User Guide](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2020-12/short-sale-volume-user-guide.pdf) and [Daily Short Sale Volume File Layout](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2021-07/DailyShortSaleVolumeFileLayout.pdf).
- **Correction:** parse at `(trade_date, symbol, market)` grain; require one-letter Market; reject duplicate symbol/facility pairs; aggregate facility rows to one symbol row in memory; keep trailer count at facility-row grain; preserve hashes and byte counts for invalid HTTP-200 payloads without storing raw market data.
