# Phase 28C2 — SEC 13D/13G quarterly-index census

## Motivation
Phase 28C issuer-resolution correctly parsed SEC submission headers but found zero filings in 2025Q4 when relying on `submissions.json` historical coverage. That is a data-coverage anomaly, not evidence of no filings.

## Deterministic correction
Use the SEC EDGAR quarterly `form.idx` indexes for Q4 2020 through Q4 2025 as the filing census. Filter first by filer CIK belonging to the frozen 335-name universe, then resolve the issuer only from the SEC submission `SUBJECT COMPANY` header.

## PIT rule
`filingDate` is the minimum public-availability date. No report date, transaction date, amendment date, or later metadata may be used as the knowledge timestamp.

## Gate
This phase is a mapping/data-completeness audit only. No returns or alpha are estimated. The 13D/13G hypothesis remains untested until the quarterly census is complete and issuer mapping is audited by period.

## Frozen baseline
F80_TOP20_PARTIAL50_BASELINE_C20 remains untouched.
