However it also restricts copying/modifying/creating derivative works, prohibits third-party disclosure, and requires data/derived works to be expunged within 30 days after termination. ThetaData's OPRA fee guide distinguishes professional display from non-display algorithmic/risk/portfolio systems; the project's automated research classification is unresolved. Written vendor/OPRA clarification is required before purchase or sample.
- The historical API is symbol-specific; trade/quote multi-day requests are limited to one month and require an expiration. General request sizing recommends one week for option tick data (one day for highly liquid names), one month for EOD, and keeping responses under one million ticks. Pro has eight account-wide concurrent requests; Standard four. The default queue is 16 and can return 429 if exceeded.
- For 335 names × 14 years, a lower-bound one-month-per-symbol EOD/OI plan implies 56,280 symbol-month requests per endpoint before retries/gaps/expiration splits. Trade/quote history can require materially more. Flat files are professional-only and self-service history is limited to seven recent calendar days; historical bulk requires sales.
- Option EOD is generated around 17:15 ET; OI is normally reported around 06:30 ET and represents the previous trading day's close. Trade/quote history pairs OPRA prints with NBBO; exclusive=true can require a quote timestamp strictly before the trade. These are event/report clocks, not actual subscriber first-receipt timestamps. Historical delivery vintages and first receipt are not certified.
- Candidate role: conditional OPRA option trade/quote, OI, EOD and Greeks/IV source. It does not identify beneficial owners, customer/firm categories, or opening/closing intent. Aggressor side would be an estimate from trade vs pre-trade NBBO and must exclude ambiguous prints.
